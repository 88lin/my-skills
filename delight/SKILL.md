Identify opportunities to add moments of joy, personality, and unexpected polish that transform functional interfaces into delightful experiences.

## Register

Brand: delight can be distributed through copy voice, section transitions, discovery rewards, seasonal touches, and personality across the surface.

Product: delight belongs at specific moments: completion, first-time actions, error recovery, milestone crossings. Reliability and consistency carry the rest.

## Assess Delight Opportunities

Find natural delight moments:

- Success states.
- Empty states and first-time experiences.
- Loading states.
- Achievements and milestones.
- Hover, click, drag, and other interactions.
- Errors where empathy matters.
- Easter eggs for curious users.

Understand brand personality, audience, emotional context, and what is appropriate. Banking is not gaming; professional can still be warm.

**CRITICAL**: Delight should enhance usability, never obscure it.

## Delight Principles

### Delight Amplifies, Never Blocks
- Keep delight moments quick.
- Never delay core functionality.
- Make delight skippable or subtle.
- Respect task focus.

### Surprise and Discovery
- Hide details for discovery.
- Reward exploration.
- Do not announce every delightful thing.

### Appropriate to Context
- Match the emotional moment.
- Respect serious or high-stakes states.
- Match brand and audience.

### Compound Over Time
- Vary responses.
- Reveal deeper layers with continued use.
- Avoid one animation that annoys on repeat.

## Techniques

### Micro-Interactions & Animation
- Satisfying button press and hover.
- Loading states with product-specific messages.
- Checkmark or success animation.
- Icon motion on hover/click.
- Drag/drop lift and snap.

### Personality in Copy
- Use product-specific, brand-appropriate language.
- Avoid cliched AI filler like "Herding pixels" or "Teaching robots to dance".
- Be warm during errors, not wacky in critical moments.

### Illustrations & Visual Personality
- Custom empty/error/loading illustrations.
- Icons with consistent personality.
- Background effects when they reinforce brand.

### Progress & Celebration
- Confetti only for meaningful milestones.
- Progress bars that celebrate completion.
- First-time actions get special treatment.
- Personalized milestone messages.

### Sound and Easter Eggs
- Use sound only when appropriate, subtle, mutable, and user-respecting.
- Easter eggs should reward curiosity without blocking tasks.

**NEVER**:
- Delay core functionality.
- Force delight.
- Use delight to hide poor UX.
- Overdo it.
- Ignore accessibility or performance.
- Make every interaction delightful.
- Be inappropriate for context.

## Verify Delight Quality

- Do users smile or share screenshots?
- Is it still pleasant after repeated use?
- Does it avoid blocking work?
- Is it performant and accessible?
- Does it match brand and context?

Delight should enhance, never obstruct.
